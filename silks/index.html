Handloom or power loom?

An honest look at the difference, so you can choose what suits the occasion and the budget.

Handloom

Process
Woven by hand on a pit or frame loom, thread by thread.
Time
One week to two months for a single saree.
Feel
Soft, slightly uneven, with small signs of the maker.
Price
Higher, as it carries weeks of skilled work.
Best for
Weddings, heirlooms, milestone gifts.

Power loom

Process
Woven by machine from the same traditional designs.
Time
A day or two per saree.
Feel
Even, crisp and consistent from piece to piece.
Price
Accessible, without leaving silk behind.
Best for
Festivals, office wear, everyday elegance.
